Overall DQ Score ⓘ how scored?
How the Overall score is calculated

Each of the 15 KPIs is scored 0–100 against its target:

● Meets or beats target → 100 pts
● Misses (higher-is-better KPI) → actual ÷ target × 100
● Exceeds (lower-is-better KPI) → 100 − overshoot × 10

Overall = average of all 15 KPI scores.

The Pillar scores (Join, Completeness…) use a softer lens — they show the proportion of clean records in each dimension, which is why they appear higher.
